Short Biography

Md Wahidur Rahman is a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Texas A&M University–Kingsville and a Student Member of IEEE. His research focuses on artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, intrusion detection, cross-platform malware analysis, federated learning, privacy-preserving AI, and explainable machine learning. His current doctoral research explores robust AI-driven frameworks for network intrusion and Windows/Android malware detection. He has contributed to research in cybersecurity, medical AI, computer vision, and intelligent IoT systems, with publications in peer-reviewed journals and international conferences. His broader goal is to develop trustworthy, secure, and practical AI solutions for real-world applications.
